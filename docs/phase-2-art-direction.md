# Phase 2 art direction: Material / interval

The opening treats architecture as a sequence of thresholds. A dark, full-bleed image field gives the first viewport depth; an oversized editorial headline crosses the boundary between image and quiet mineral paper. Fine coordinates, figure numbers and ruled lines behave like drawing annotations, while the kiln accent marks only selected actions.

The image enters through a short vertical mask reveal and settles with a restrained scale change. Type arrives in a compact stagger, with no blocking intro. Navigation opens as a warm paper plane carrying large indexed links. Its native modal dialog owns focus containment and background inertness.

The custom cursor is supplemental and limited to fine pointers with motion enabled. It follows via one animation frame loop, labels genuinely explorable imagery, and magnetic movement is limited to the hero action and navigation visual-study link. Native focus and editable text remain intact.

On tablet the image and type move into separate planes. On mobile the display scale, image height and layering simplify; navigation remains a touch-sized modal and all content remains visible without hover. The hero headline and supporting copy are provisional. Architectural imagery and the visual-study sequence are explicitly demonstration material.
